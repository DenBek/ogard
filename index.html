The engineering problem

One physical asset can have several valid information identities

Maintenance systems, asset registers, telemetry historians, work orders, inspections, and technical documents often describe the same equipment through different identifiers and time frames. OGARD preserves those records as evidence, reconciles them without erasing disagreement, and records the basis for each material decision.

01

Functional location

The role or position in the network. It can remain stable when physical equipment is replaced.

02

Equipment unit

The physical installed item. It can be replaced, removed, relocated, or associated with a serial-number history.

03

Telemetry identity

The tags or channels used to observe an asset context. They can be renamed, re-pointed, duplicated, or left stale.

Reference architecture

Four logical layers, with evidence and authority kept visible

OGARD separates raw evidence, conformance decisions, the canonical asset-and-evidence layer, and controlled consumption. Review, contradiction, and audit operate as cross-cutting controls.

  • Preserve source evidence before interpretation
  • Apply deterministic rules before similarity methods
  • Route ambiguity and consequential cases to review
  • Keep operator authority explicit

Broader methodology design

Designed to preserve evidence, uncertainty, and review

Traceable mappings

Accepted, rejected, deferred, and unresolved relationships retain their evidence, rule or score, version, actor, and time.

Typed contradictions

Material disagreement remains visible and routable rather than being silently overwritten through survivorship.

Authorized review

Human review is a designed control for ambiguous or consequential cases and is recorded within the data model.

Controlled Unknown

Where the available evidence does not support another status, Unknown records that state explicitly.
