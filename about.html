About

OGARD is designed to make grid-asset evidence reconciliation more inspectable, auditable, and explicit about uncertainty and decision authority.

Author

Deniz Bektas is a senior data engineer who specializes in reconciling fragmented asset, maintenance and operational records into governed, decision-grade data. His professional work spans reconciliation engines, event-driven ingestion and governed lakehouse architecture, including work for energy-sector organizations.

He conceived OGARD and is the author of its four technical papers and the executable reference implementation.

He holds an MSc in Computer Science with Distinction from Birkbeck, University of London, and is a Databricks Certified Data Engineer Professional.

The project

OGARD is an independent open project. Its papers, diagrams, examples and code are original work. The worked example and test cases use synthetic records, and the papers draw on public standards and literature.

Papers, diagrams and source code are versioned together with their licenses and development record, and each release states its version and scope.
