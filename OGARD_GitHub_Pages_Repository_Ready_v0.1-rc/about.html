About OGARD

An independently authored technical reference methodology

OGARD is designed to make grid-asset evidence reconciliation more inspectable, auditable, and explicit about uncertainty and decision authority.

Author

Deniz Bektas

Deniz Bektas is a data engineer and the creator of OGARD. He holds an MSc in Computer Science and works on auditable data-platform design, governed integration, and the translation of fragmented operational records into traceable information products.

OGARD is independently authored and is presented as a reusable engineering method rather than an employer product, client implementation, or utility-endorsed standard.

Preserve evidence before interpretation. Route uncertainty rather than hiding it. Keep operator authority explicit.

OGARD design principles

Independence and confidentiality

Public artifacts use only original, synthetic, or publicly available material

No protected operational data

The public package contains no non-public utility records, credentials, operator configurations, or confidential operating information.

No employer or client material

The package contains no employer source code, client data, proprietary product architecture, or client-specific implementation detail.

No endorsement implication

References to standards, technology categories, or professional experience do not imply organizational sponsorship, certification, or adoption.
