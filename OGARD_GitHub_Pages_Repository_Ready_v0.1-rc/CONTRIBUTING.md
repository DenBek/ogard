# Contributing and Technical Feedback

OGARD welcomes focused technical feedback on the published methodology and artifacts.

## Good issue topics

- internal inconsistencies or unclear terminology
- errors in the worked example or structured records
- proposed contradiction-taxonomy changes
- conformance-control improvements
- reproducibility or accessibility defects
- documented implementation observations that can be shared publicly

## Do not submit

- credentials or security-sensitive configuration
- employer, client, or operator-controlled records
- non-public utility data or operational details
- personal data
- claims of adoption, performance, or reliability impact without specific supporting evidence

## Issue format

Identify the exact document or artifact version, the relevant section or field, the technical observation, and a proposed correction where possible.

Submissions may be discussed, revised, declined, or deferred. Opening an issue does not transfer authorship of OGARD or establish endorsement of the framework.
